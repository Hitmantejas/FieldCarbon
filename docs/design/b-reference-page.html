Fleet overview, May 2025 to April 2026

Three sites, 713 jobs. Every figure is traceable; hover it, and the margin shows how we got there.

The fleet burned 16,493 litres1 of red diesel, which is 55.8 tonnes of CO2e2 using UK government figures for 2025, including the fuel's production and delivery.

The arable site in Bavaria used 10,814 litres3, two thirds of the total. Part of that was burned outside jobs: idling, road travel and tools attached but not working.

We found three things worth a look. One recorder repeated the same values for 92 hours4, so real use may be higher. A depot's book stock differs from its measured stock by about 900 litres5.

Switching part of the fuel to HVO or reducing tillage could cut emissions, but only research ranges back these. They are listed below; ideas overlap and are never added together.
