# Surface brief: FieldCarbon dashboard (frontend/)

Mode: Operate. Primary visitor: farmer on a phone. Secondary: hackathon judges on a laptop recording a 3-minute demo.
Job: see where machinery fuel goes, what it costs and emits, and how sure we are. Flow: Your farm, Pain points, Goal, Plan, Sharpen, Report.
Proof on hand: real Farm A aggregates via the API. No Farm B, brand assets or testimonials; do not fabricate.
Unresolved: typefaces and exact palette values are the builder's call; Farm A/B toggle waits for Farm B.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dashboard is the delivery docket for your farm's fuel, printed as you read it. It refuses the card-grid SaaS dashboard with a hero metric and a row of icon tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Cool thermal-paper white with black print; hi-vis highlighter lime used only as a marker behind the one figure that matters. Dark theme is amber print on a black roll. Confidence labels are rubber stamps with a text word plus an outline form (solid, dashed, hollow), never colour alone. Ranges are bracketed bars. Rows are separated by perforation rules and a torn edge; condensed industrial display numerals, a hyperlegible sans for prose, a tabular mono for measurement only.

STORY: The farmer understands within seconds how much fuel the farm used, what it cost as a range, and how much was not doing work, and can see how sure each number is. They believe it because every figure opens its working. They tap Next to reach pain points.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone width, one column on a docket strip. Top: demo-data strip, then FIELDCARBON and FARM A. Below: the fuel total as one giant condensed numeral cropped past the right edge with a MEASURED stamp, then CO2e and the cost range bar with its ESTIMATED stamp, then the share of fuel not doing work as a printed bar. The primary action, a Next: Pain points bar, sits at the thumb at the bottom.

FORM: Fuel delivery docket (thermal receipt), seed key 7bc9a4df, assigned third on the grounded list. Raises: line-form state from the emission-line rail, tape-flip for the farm toggle from the cassette j-card (deferred until Farm B), giant cropped numeral from the metro tiles.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
